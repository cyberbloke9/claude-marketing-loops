## Signal 1: Hyderabad builders sold flats before registering the project — and TG-RERA just published the receipts

- **What the data shows:** Between 6 and 13 Aug 2026, TG-RERA issued a fresh batch of penalty orders against Hyderabad-area developers for selling before RERA registration. In an order dated 6 Aug 2026, TG-RERA fined Adhuri Infra Pvt Ltd ₹6,77,966 for collecting 25% of the sale consideration from a homebuyer without executing a registered Agreement for Sale, ruling the promoter "cannot benefit from wrong." Reported together on 13 Aug 2026 (The Hans India), the same enforcement cycle penalised AV Infracon Pvt Ltd ₹2,83,140 (AV East Residency) and ₹14,84,184 (AV West Heights) for carrying out sales without mandatory RERA registration, and levied ₹43,16,480 on the Tech Homes project (developer: SVM Aditya Homes) for selling units with neither RERA registration nor the required HMDA approval in place. Sources: [Newsmeter, 'TGRERA fines Adhuri Infra ₹6.78L', order dated 6 Aug 2026](https://newsmeter.in/hyderabad/cannot-benefit-from-wrong-tgrera-fines-adhuri-infra-678l-for-accepting-25-money-sans-registration-773392), [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/tg-rera-fines-builder-rs-6-78-lakh-for-violating-homebuyer-rules-3522637/), [The Hans India, 'TG-RERA acts tough, slaps heavy penalty on rogue developers in city', 13 Aug 2026](https://www.thehansindia.com/news/cities/hyderabad/tg-rera-acts-tough-slaps-heavy-penalty-on-rogue-developers-in-city-1108837).
- **Why now:** the freshest, most specific Hyderabad-only RERA item in three loops — the last two reports explicitly left the Hyderabad slot "open" beyond the pan-India extension thread; this sweep finally surfaces a distinct, dated, multi-project enforcement cycle with real rupee figures attached to named projects.
- **Exclusivity:** LOW-MEDIUM (public regulatory orders) — TERREM's synthesis: every one of these penalties describes the exact failure mode a buyer can check for themselves in two minutes — money collected, or units marketed, before a project ever had a RERA number or (in Tech Homes' case) an HMDA approval. The checkable takeaway is blunt: verify RERA registration and HMDA approval *before* the token payment, not after, because the "penalty" a buyer reads about later doesn't undo the money already collected.
- **Persona × pain:** P1×QUALITY/DELIVERY (buying into a project that was never legally cleared to sell), P2×RERA (concentrated enforcement pattern — four projects, one city, one week — as a builder-risk signal)

## Signal 2: The four-month builder excuse just became a national map — Tamil Nadu and Haryana joined this week, and now every major TERREM city has its own order

- **What the data shows:** Two more states operationalised the 31 Jul 2026 MoHUA force-majeure advisory this sweep. TNRERA's circular dated 10 Aug 2026 automatically extends the completion timeline by four months for every Tamil Nadu project whose original, revised, or extended completion date falls on or after 28 Feb 2026 — no separate application or fee required. HRERA Gurugram followed on 11 Aug 2026 with the same four-month automatic extension for Haryana-registered projects meeting the same 28 Feb 2026 eligibility window, but added a harder cutoff than Maharashtra's: the benefit is confined to projects registered on or before 31 Jul 2026, with the extension itself granted on or before that date. Both orders invoke Section 6 (force majeure) of the RERA Act and the Finance Ministry's April 2026 classification of the West Asia conflict as "war." With Maharashtra (7 Aug) and Telangana already covered, this closes the Chennai and NCR/Gurugram slots explicitly left open on the last two loops' watchlists — TERREM's audience now has a five-state comparison, not a two-state one. Sources: [Aparna Decors, TNRERA circular, 10 Aug 2026](https://www.aparnadecors.com/2026/08/tamil-nadu-rera-gives-four-month-relief.html), [Verified.RealEstate community, TNRERA](https://community.verified.realestate/article/tnrera-gives-builders-4-more-months-what-changes-for-tamil-nadu-homebuyers/), [The Tribune, HRERA order, 11 Aug 2026](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/haryana-rera-extends-real-estate-project-deadlines-by-4-months-over-west-asia-war/), [Business Standard, HRERA](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/haryana-rera-grants-four-month-project-extension-amid-west-asia-crisis-126081201372_1.html).
- **Why now:** direct continuation of the thread flagged reactive on 27 Jul and followed through on 10 Aug — but this loop is the first time Chennai and NCR/Gurugram (both explicitly "open" on the last two watchlists) get their own dated, state-specific order rather than sitting under the generic pan-India advisory.

## Signal 3: While Bengaluru's building approvals stalled, Delhi just approved a plan for 20 lakh new homes and deregulated its own approval process

- **What the data shows:** On 12 Aug 2026, the Delhi Development Authority approved the draft Master Plan for Delhi-2047, replacing the earlier Master Plan-2041 draft and targeting roughly 20 lakh new housing units to accommodate a projected population of 3.2 crore by 2047 (up from ~2.4 crore today); the plan also opens the door to redeveloping nearly 4 lakh DDA flats that are over 50 years old. Alongside the master plan, DDA approved amendments to the Unified Building Bye-Laws (UBBL)-2016 that de-link prior NOCs from the Delhi Pollution Control Committee, the Chief Inspector of Factories, Delhi Jal Board, and the Forest Department from the building-permit process — explicitly aimed at cutting approval time. The draft now goes to MoHUA for final notification. Sources: [ANI, 'DDA approves Delhi Master Plan 2047', 12 Aug 2026](https://www.aninews.in/news/national/general-news/dda-approves-delhi-master-plan-2047-to-guide-capitals-future-growth-sent-for-centres-nod20260812172753/), [India TV News, 12–13 Aug 2026](https://www.indiatvnews.com/business/news/dda-approves-20-lakh-new-homes-village-urbanisation-under-delhi-master-plan-2047-2026-08-13-1051239), [NoBroker, DDA flat redevelopment](https://www.nobroker.in/news/delhi/dda-approves-delhi-master-plan-2047-allows-redevelopment-of-50-year-old-dda-homes/).

## Signal 4: India's unsold housing stock just crossed 5.25 lakh units — and nearly all the growth is happening in homes most buyers can't afford

- **What the data shows:** Knight Frank India's H1 2026 report (released 8 Aug 2026) puts unsold residential inventory across eight major markets at 5,25,695 units as of end-H1 2026, up 4% YoY. The composition shift is sharp: inventory in the sub-₹50 lakh segment fell 7% YoY to 1,71,363 units and the ₹50 lakh–₹1 crore segment fell 3% to 1,34,841 units, while the ₹1–2 crore segment rose 12% YoY, the ₹2–5 crore segment jumped 43% YoY to 65,671 units, and the ₹5–10 crore segment rose 23% YoY. On liquidity, NCR posted the worst Quarters-to-Sell (QTS) ratio at 7.6 quarters and Ahmedabad the worst overall at 8.1, while Pune posted the best (fastest-clearing) at 4.0 quarters. Separately, a parallel Aug 2026 report (Aparna Decors, citing brokerage estimates) has India's top four listed developers — DLF, Godrej Properties, Prestige Estates, and Lodha — targeting a combined ₹1.19 lakh crore in FY27 pre-sales, weighted toward further premium and luxury launches. Sources: [ANI, Knight Frank unsold-inventory report, 8 Aug 2026](https://aninews.in/news/business/unsold-housing-inventory-rises-4-yoy-to-over-525-lakh-units-in-h1-2026-knight-frank20260808160840/), [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/unsold-housing-inventory-rises-4-yoy-to-over-5-25-lakh-units-in-h1-2026-knight-frank/), [Asianet Newsable](https://newsable.asianetnews.com/business/unsold-homes-in-india-climb-to-525-695-units-premium-stock-rises-articleshow-446zy6u), [Aparna Decors, developer FY27 pipeline](https://www.aparnadecors.com/2026/08/top-11-indian-developers-target-182.html).
- **Why now:** this is a new, dated market-report release (8 Aug 2026) inside this sweep's window — exactly the "new market report" brief item — and it lands the same week the biggest listed developers confirmed they're leaning further into the same premium segment where unsold stock is already growing fastest.
- **Exclusivity:** LOW (public consultancy report, third-party analysed) — TERREM's synthesis: most "unsold inventory" headlines report one national number; this data shows the growth is concentrated almost entirely above ₹1 crore (+12% to +43% YoY) while affordable and mid-segment stock is actually *shrinking* — which reads as good news only until you notice it means builders have largely stopped building for the affordable buyer, not that affordable demand is being satisfied. The city-level QTS split (NCR's 7.6 quarters to clear stock vs Pune's 4.0) is the checkable version of "which city is actually easy to exit" for a P2 investor.

## Signal 5: The Supreme Court's deadline on a 132-acre Mumbai redevelopment arrived this week — and the buried number is a transit rent set below market, unilaterally

- **What the data shows:** The Supreme Court's 29 Jul 2026 stay on MHADA's cluster redevelopment of Bandra Reclamation (98.27 acres) and Adarsh Nagar/Worli (34.33 acres) — roughly 132.6 acres combined, first covered in this report's 3 Aug edition — reached its scheduled hearing date of 13 Aug 2026 this week, with Maharashtra, MHADA and other respondents having been directed to file counter-affidavits within a week of the stay order. The petitioning cooperative housing societies' filings, per LiveLaw and Moneylife's reporting on the case, argue that MHADA unilaterally fixed transit rent at ₹75,000/month and a rehabilitation corpus of ₹30 lakh for affected buildings without consultation or a market-rate survey, and that both figures sit well below prevailing Bandra Reclamation market rates — the societies argue this deprives them of the notice, hearing, and fair-compensation safeguards attached to their property rights. No outcome from the 13 Aug hearing itself is yet reported as of this sweep. Sources: [Moneylife, 29 Jul 2026 stay order](https://www.moneylife.in/article/sc-stays-work-order-for-mhadas-bandra-reclamation-cluster-redevelopment-seeks-states-reply/81224.html), [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/top-stories/supreme-court-restrains-mhada-from-issuing-work-order-for-bandra-redevelopment-project-543719), [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/no-work-to-be-issued-for-proposed-cluster-redevelopment-project-at-bandra-reclamation-till-aug-13-sc-to-mhada).
