## Signal 1: The Supreme Court just told a bankrupt builder: fix this, or we take your company

- **What the data shows:** On Tuesday, 8 September 2026, a Supreme Court bench of Chief Justice of India Surya Kant and Justices Joymalya Bagchi and V Mohana rejected a proposal from Parsvnath Developers Ltd for resolving the claims of hundreds of distressed homebuyers, calling it "another device" to delay possession and repayment. The Court warned that unless Parsvnath files a fresh proposal satisfying the claims of all allottees across the company and its subsidiaries, it will be "constrained to appoint a High-Powered Committee for entrustment of all the responsibilities" — with the CJI floating a ₹500 crore deposit with the Registry as the trigger point for that takeover. Parsvnath's counsel told the Court the group was contracted to build 27,000 units across 24 housing projects; 24,000 have been sold, and around 3,000 remain undelivered. The company entered Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process at the NCLT, New Delhi (insolvency commencement date 30 April 2026, order received 12 May 2026, IRP Manoj Kumar Anand); the Resolution Professional has since received roughly 3,000 homebuyer claims worth an estimated ₹800 crore — covering refunds, delayed-possession compensation, and RERA orders the company never executed. The Court specifically cited the case of Rita Tikku, a cancer survivor, and Lokaish Tikku, who put their life savings into Parsvnath Exotica in Sector 53, Gurugram — an NCR project. Sources: [Deccan Herald, "Supreme Court rejects Parsvnath Developers' proposal on homebuyers, warns of high-powered committee", 8 Sept 2026](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/supreme-court-rejects-parsvnath-developers-proposal-on-homebuyers-warns-of-high-powered-committee-4139225), [Verdictum, "Another Device To Delay Possession & Repayment: Supreme Court Rejects Proposal Of Parsvnath Developers For Homebuyers"](https://www.verdictum.in/amp/supreme-court/proposal-of-parsvnath-developers-for-homebuyers-rejected-by-sc-1621560), [The CSR Journal, "Supreme Court Rejects Parsvnath Proposal, Warns of Tougher Action"](https://thecsrjournal.in/supreme-court-rejects-parsvnath-proposal-warns-of-tougher-action), [Rail 24 News, "SC rejects proposal of Parsvnath Developers for homebuyers, asked it to file another"](https://rail24.in/news/investment/sc-rejects-proposal-of-parsvnath-developers-for-homebuyers-asked-it-to-file-anot).
- **Why now:** the order landed 8 Sept 2026, inside this sweep — and it is the second straight loop the Supreme Court has moved decisively on who bears the cost of a builder collapse (last loop's Signal 1: the 3 Sept Granite Gate/Noida ruling on CIRP-cost liability). ## Signal 5: Developer margins just fell — not because business is bad, but because fewer buyers got the cheap price

- **What the data shows:** A Nuvama Research note published 8 September 2026 found that Indian developers' cash EBITDA margins declined to 39% in FY26 from 42% in FY25, attributing the drop mainly to a lower proportion of inventory being sold at the launch stage — historically the cheapest pricing tier a project offers. Nuvama frames this as evidence the sector is entering the "middle" of its current cycle: balance sheets are now stronger and leverage lower than in prior cycles, debt (rather than fresh equity) has become the primary funding source for growth, and developers are shifting capital from debt reduction toward land acquisition and annuity-generating assets. The report expects margins to stabilise from here as sales momentum and launch absorption hold steady. Sources: [ANI, "Residential realty enters middle cycle as focus shifts to capital deployment, annuity assets: Nuvama", 8 Sept 2026](https://aninews.in/news/business/residential-realty-enters-middle-cycle-as-focus-shifts-to-capital-deployment-annuity-assets-nuvama20260908111916/), [The Tribune, same report, 8 Sept 2026](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/annuity-capex/residential-realty-enters-middle-cycle-as-focus-shifts-to-capital-deployment-annuity-assets-nuvama), [Punjab Kesari English, same report](https://english.punjabkesari.com/business/residential-realty-enters-middle-cycle-as-focus-shifts-to-capital-deployment-annuity-assets-nuvama).
- **Why now:** published 8 September 2026, inside this sweep — the freshest pan-India, brokerage-sourced financial data point on developer health this loop, and a genuine counter-read to the assumption that a margin decline signals distress.
- **Exclusivity:** LOW (public sell-side research note) — TERREM's synthesis: a 3-point margin drop sounds like a warning sign. Nuvama's own attribution says otherwise — it's a mix-shift, not a demand or cost problem: fewer units are moving at launch-stage pricing, meaning more buyers are paying later-stage (higher) prices rather than developers discounting to sell. For a buyer counting on an early-bird discount, that's the quieter version of "the discount ends when the tower does" — the launch-stage price window itself is shrinking as a share of total sales, independent of any single project's construction timeline.
- **Persona × pain:** P1×PRICE (the launch-stage discount window — long treated as a reliable entry point — is structurally shrinking across the sector, not just at any one project), P2×EXIT (lower leverage and debt-funded, cash-flow-generating annuity assets is a genuine reduction in sector-wide developer counterparty risk, relevant to anyone evaluating builder financial stability before committing capital)
- **Suggested hook direction:** #4 (loss aversion — scarcity tied to construction stage), adapted — "Developer margins fell 3 points this year. Not because business is bad — because fewer buyers got the launch-stage price. The cheap window is shrinking, sector-wide."
- **Flywheel target:** intel.terrem.in/markets
