## Signal 3: A Hyderabad flat was sold twice — once on a registered deed, once on a Power of Attorney. TG-RERA just ruled the paper trail, not the story, wins.

- **What the data shows:** In an order dated 21 Jul 2026, TG-RERA members K. Srinivasa Rao and Laxmi Narayana Jannu directed Aparna Constructions and Estates Pvt Ltd to hand over Apartment 403, Block J, in its "Aparna Sarovar Zicon" project (Nallagandla, Hyderabad) to Banjara Hills resident Shridevi Valluri within 30 days. The apartment had first been sold via a registered sale deed to Koshy Mathew and Shiney Mathew in December 2023; it was later sold again to Valluri through a registered deed executed under an irrevocable General Power of Attorney (GPA) held by a third party. TG-RERA held that a registered sale deed is presumed valid unless a competent court sets it aside — ordering possession transferred to the second buyer, while explicitly keeping the order subject to the outcome of pending civil and insolvency proceedings over the title. Sources: [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/tg-rera-asks-aparna-constructions-to-hand-over-hyderabad-flat-3512161/), [Newsmeter](https://newsmeter.in/hyderabad/civil-dispute-can-wait-tgrera-asks-aparna-constructions-to-hand-over-flat-in-nallagandla-project-772139), [Hyderabad Mail](https://hyderabadmail.com/tg-rera-orders-aparna-constructions-hand-over-nallagandla-flat-title-dispute/), order dated 21 Jul 2026.
