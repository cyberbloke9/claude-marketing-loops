## Signal 1: Listed developers' bookings just fell 21% nationally — but the story isn't collapsing demand, it's builders who stopped launching

- **What the data shows:** Sales bookings ("pre-sales") of India's 28 major listed real estate firms fell 21% year-on-year to ₹39,964 crore in Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026), down from ₹50,900 crore a year earlier — with the "announced universe" of pre-sales down ~24% YoY and ~26% quarter-on-quarter, per analyst estimates reported 16 Aug 2026. The decline is sharply uneven: DLF's bookings collapsed 94.24% to just ₹657 crore (from ₹11,425 crore) because it launched zero new projects in the quarter; Prestige Estates fell 46% to ₹6,579 crore against a high base from its Indirapuram launch a year earlier; Oberoi Realty fell 35.93% to ₹1,049.88 crore against its Elysian Tower launch base. Meanwhile Godrej Properties rose 22% to ₹8,651 crore (from ₹7,082 crore) to become the largest listed developer by bookings, and Lodha grew 4% to ₹4,629 crore despite limited launches of its own — 19 of the 28 tracked developers actually grew pre-sales YoY. Sources: [Business Standard, "Real estate sales soften on fewer launches and high base in Q1FY27", 16 Aug 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/real-estate-sales-soften-on-fewer-launches-and-high-base-in-q1fy27-126081600279_1.html), [Business Standard, "Sales bookings of listed realty firms drop 21% amid fewer project launches", 23 Aug 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/sales-bookings-of-listed-realty-firms-drop-21-amid-fewer-project-launches-126082300359_1.html), [Free Press Journal, "Realty Firms Sales Bookings Fall 21% To ₹40,000 Crore, Godrej Properties Tops June Quarter Chart"](https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/realty-firms-sales-bookings-fall-21-to-40000-crore-godrej-properties-tops-june-quarter-chart), [Whalesbook, "India Realty Sales Bookings Fall 21% in Q1; Godrej Properties Leads"](https://www.whalesbook.com/news/English/real-estate/India-Realty-Sales-Bookings-Fall-21percent-in-Q1-Godrej-Properties-Leads/6a8ac63c84d2dd5c12e192c0).
- **Why now:** the freshest pan-India market-structure data point this sweep — the headline number landed 23 Aug 2026, a day before this report, and it directly updates the "waiting is safe" question this cohort keeps asking.
- **Exclusivity:** LOW (public, analyst-compiled listed-company data) — TERREM's synthesis: a 21% national drop reads like a demand story until you see it's actually a launch-supply story split almost perfectly by company — the developers who paused new launches (DLF, Prestige, Oberoi, all against high prior-year-launch bases) cratered, while the two that kept launching (Godrej, Lodha) grew. For a buyer, that means the "cooling market" headline is really "fewer new options this quarter," which is the opposite of a signal to keep waiting — supply pulled back, it didn't get cheaper.
- **Persona × pain:** P1×PRICE (the 81% price-anxiety cohort reading "sales fell 21%" as a reason to wait, when it's actually a launch-supply squeeze), P2×YIELD (which developers are still deploying capital — Godrej/Lodha vs DLF/Prestige/Oberoi pulling back — is itself a builder-risk and allocation signal)

## Signal 2: Bengaluru's discounted path to a legal Khata just closed — literally the day before this report

- **What the data shows:** Under the Karnataka government's "Bhu Guarantee" ("Nanna e-Khata, Nanna Hakku") drive, the betterment fee to convert an eligible B-Khata property to a legal A-Khata was cut from 5% to 2% of guidance value for a 100-day window running 15 May–23 Aug 2026; from 24 Aug 2026 the fee reverts to the full 5%. On a property with a ₹1 crore guidance value, that difference is roughly ₹3 lakh. Eligibility was limited to B-Khata properties registered on or before 30 September 2024, with all property-tax dues cleared, direct access to a public road, and no major encroachment or structural violations; conversions were processed online via the GBA/BBMP e-Aasthi portal with a 15-day target for clean applications, and roughly 60,000 conversions were approved under the drive as of this window. Sources: [PKP Advocates, "B-Khata to A-Khata in Bengaluru: 2% Fee Deadline 23 August 2026"](https://www.pkpadvocates.in/blog/b-khata-to-a-khata-conversion-bengaluru), [Landeed, "B-Khata to A-Khata Conversion in Karnataka"](https://www.landeed.com/post/b-khata-to-a-khata-conversion-in-karnataka), [OneCityProperty, "B-Khata to A-Khata at 2%: Deadline 23 August, Act Now"](https://www.onecityproperty.com/news/bhu-guarantee-b-khata-deadline-bangalore), [PropNewz, 23 Jul 2026](https://www.propnewz.com/blog/b-khata-a-khata-conversion-one-time-settlement-bengaluru-buyers-2026-07-23).
- **Why now:** the discount window's own closing date (23 Aug 2026) fell exactly one day before this report — the freshest possible "why now" a regulatory deadline can have, and it finally gives Bengaluru a distinct, dated item after three straight loops where the city's registration/regulatory slot ran on the approval-logjam story instead.
- **Exclusivity:** LOW (public government scheme) — TERREM's synthesis: most coverage of this drive framed it as a one-time discount to grab; the sharper, less-covered angle is what it implies going forward — a B-Khata property is a checkable legal-status problem (restricted resale, weaker loan eligibility, no automatic path to occupancy certification) that just got 3x more expensive to fix as of yesterday, for anyone who didn't act inside the window.
- **Persona × pain:** P1×QUALITY (a B-Khata property carries hidden legal-status risk most buyers don't verify before purchase), P2×EXIT (B-Khata status directly restricts resale and loan eligibility — a liquidity problem for an owned asset, now costlier to fix)
- **Suggested hook direction:** #1 family (loss aversion, cost of inaction quantified), adapted — "Bengaluru just let a 3 lakh rupee window on your Khata status close. If you own a B-Khata property, fixing it now costs 5% instead of 2% of guidance value. Here's how to check your Khata status in 2 minutes."

## Signal 3: A Hyderabad broker took ₹52 lakh for two plots, bounced the refund cheques — TG-RERA just ordered the money back

- **What the data shows:** In an order dated 17 Aug 2026, TG-RERA members K Srinivasa Rao and Laxmi Narayana Jannu directed a Hyderabad real estate consultancy firm to refund ₹42.2 lakh to two plot buyers within 60 days. The buyers had approached the firm in May 2022 for plots 229 and 230 in Sree Rugveda Brundavanam Hills and paid ₹52.2 lakh by 7 June 2022; ₹10 lakh was eventually returned, but cheques issued for the remaining ₹42.2 lakh were dishonoured. Sources: [Siasat, "TG RERA orders Rs 42.2 lakh refund to Hyderabad plot buyers"](https://www.siasat.com/tg-rera-orders-rs-42-2-lakh-refund-to-hyderabad-plot-buyers-3528367/), [Urban Acres, "Hyderabad Real Estate Buyers Get RERA Relief"](https://urbanacres.in/hyderabad-real-estate-buyers-get-rera-relief/).
- **Why now:** the order landed 17 Aug 2026, inside this sweep's window — the third straight loop with a Hyderabad TG-RERA item, but a genuinely different failure mode than the last two (builder-registration violations on 10 and 17 Aug): this is an intermediary/consultancy firm taking payment and issuing cheques that bounced, a fraud-adjacent risk most buyers associate with informal deals, not a RERA-tracked transaction.
- **Exclusivity:** LOW-MEDIUM (public regulatory order) — TERREM's synthesis: the checkable failure here predates the bounced cheques — two years elapsed between the 2022 payment and this 2026 order, meaning the buyers held dishonoured cheques for an extended period before TG-RERA forced the money back. The takeaway a buyer can act on immediately: never accept a post-dated or delayed-refund cheque from an intermediary in lieu of an executed, registered agreement — RERA can eventually order the money back, but "eventually" here took over two years.

## Signal 4: Panvel's prices just outran the rest of Navi Mumbai — 76% up in five years, on ANAROCK's own index

On ANAROCK's price index (2021 = 100), Panvel now stands at 176 versus 164 for the broader Navi Mumbai market — meaning Panvel has out-appreciated the region it sits inside.
