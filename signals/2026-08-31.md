## Signal 1: RBI's own price index just confirmed it — the cities driving India's "housing boom" aren't the ones TERREM's buyers are looking at

- **What the data shows:** The RBI's quarterly House Price Index (HPI), released 24 Aug 2026 and compiled from transaction-level data supplied by state registration authorities across 18 major cities, put the All-India HPI at 117.5 for Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026) — up 1.1% quarter-on-quarter from 116.2 in Q4 FY26, and up 3.6% year-on-year from 113.4 in Q1 FY26. The RBI named the cities actually driving that annual growth: Chandigarh, Jaipur, Kanpur, Lucknow and Thiruvananthapuram. None of TERREM's core coverage cities — Mumbai, Delhi/NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai or Pune, all of which sit inside the RBI's own 18-city panel — were named among the quarter's growth leaders. Sources: [ANI, "India's housing price rise 1.1% sequentially in Q1 FY27, Chandigarh, Lucknow lead gains: RBI", 24 Aug 2026](https://aninews.in/news/business/indias-housing-price-rise-11-sequentially-in-q1-fy27-chandigarh-lucknow-lead-gains-rbi20260824185719/), [ANI, "India's house price growth slows to 3.6% in Q1 FY27: RBI", 24 Aug 2026](https://aninews.in/news/business/indias-house-price-growth-slows-to-36-in-q1-fy27-rbi20260824201225/), [Devdiscourse, 24 Aug 2026](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/business/3967152-indias-house-price-growth-slows-to-36-in-q1-fy27-rbi), [Asianet Newsable](https://newsable.asianetnews.com/business/india-s-house-price-growth-slows-to-3-6-in-q1-fy27-says-rbi-articleshow-o1qqrbp).
- **Why now:** the freshest pan-India, RBI-sourced data point this sweep — released 24 Aug 2026, inside this window — and a genuinely different source class from the analyst-compiled bookings data covered in recent loops (this is registration-authority transaction data, not company-reported pre-sales).
- **Exclusivity:** LOW (public RBI release, registration-authority-sourced) — TERREM's synthesis: most coverage led with the reassuring national headline ("prices still rising"). The buried detail is which cities the RBI actually credited for that rise — a list of five cities that doesn't include a single one of TERREM's six core metros. For a buyer in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, MMR, Pune or NCR, "national house prices are up" is true and almost irrelevant — their own city's number, whatever it is, wasn't what moved the average.

## Signal 2: A Hyderabad builder took pre-launch money for flats it never built — TG-RERA just ordered 12 buyers repaid, again

- **What the data shows:** In two common orders passed Tuesday, 25 Aug 2026, TG-RERA members Laxmi Narayana Jannu and K Srinivasa Rao directed Bharathi Builders Pvt Ltd, its partners and the parties who later took over the project to jointly and severally refund all 12 complainant-buyers of "Bharathi's Lake View Apartments" (Kompally village, Medchal-Malkajgiri district), with 10.70% annual interest, within 60 days. The buyers had signed Memorandums of Understanding in 2021 under a pre-launch scheme — not a registered Agreement for Sale — on a promise of possession within 24 months plus a 6-month grace period; no construction activity was ever started. This is the same project TG-RERA had already fined Bharathi Builders ₹4.74 crore for illegally collecting pre-launch funds without RERA registration, declaring it a "defaulter promoter." Sources: [Siasat, "TGRERA orders refund, 10 pc interest for Kompally flat buyers"](https://www.siasat.com/tgrera-orders-refund-10-pc-interest-for-kompally-flat-buyers-3532052/), [NewsMeter, "Bharathi Builders leaves buyers in lurch after Kompally project fiasco; TGRERA orders refund"](https://newsmeter.in/hyderabad/hyderabad-bharathi-builders-leaves-buyers-in-lurch-after-kompally-project-fiasco-tgrera-orders-refund-767240), [Siasat, "TG RERA fines Bharathi Builders Rs 4.74 crore for illegal pre-launch offer"](https://www.siasat.com/tg-rera-fines-bharathi-builders-rs-4-74-crore-for-illegal-pre-launch-offer-3434951/).
- **Why now:** the refund order itself landed 25 Aug 2026, inside this sweep's window — the fourth straight loop with a distinct Hyderabad TG-RERA enforcement item, but this one names the exact instrument buyers signed instead of a registered contract: an MoU under a "pre-launch" scheme.
- **Exclusivity:** LOW-MEDIUM (public regulatory orders) — TERREM's synthesis: every buyer in this case paid against a Memorandum of Understanding, not a registered Agreement for Sale — the document RERA actually protects. The checkable takeaway is narrower and sharper than "check RERA registration": if what you're being asked to sign is an MoU, a "pre-launch" agreement, or anything short of a registered Agreement for Sale, RERA's protections don't attach to it until you have one, however reputable the sales pitch sounds.
- **Persona × pain:** P1×QUALITY/DELIVERY (money collected against an unregistered instrument, zero construction in four years), P2×RERA (a builder already fined once for the identical violation, still working through refund enforcement on the same project years later)
- **Suggested hook direction:** #2 family (avoidable loss + it was checkable), adapted — "A Hyderabad builder collected money on an MoU, not a registered Agreement for Sale. ## Signal 3: Pune finally gets its own RERA item — a couple waited from 2018 to 2022 for a flat, and MahaRERA just made the builder pay for the gap

- **What the data shows:** In an order dated 27 Aug 2026, MahaRERA Member II Ravindra Deshpande directed Shri Siddhivinayak Developers, promoter of Ganga Amber Phase I (Pune), to pay delayed-possession interest to homebuyers Amol Bhausaheb Bagul and Priyanka Amol Bagul. The couple booked a flat for ₹33.11 lakh and signed a registered Agreement for Sale on 23 Aug 2018, which committed to possession by 31 Dec 2020; possession was actually handed over around 18 months later. MahaRERA ordered interest for the delay period (1 Jan 2021 to 14 Jun 2022), calculated at SBI's highest MCLR plus 2%, per Rule 18 of the Maharashtra RERA Rules 2017, payable within 60 days. Source: [Pune Pulse, "Pune: MahaRERA Orders Ganga Amber Developer To Pay Interest For 18-Month Delayed Possession Of Rs 33 Lakh Flat"](https://www.mypunepulse.com/pune-maharera-orders-ganga-amber-developer-to-pay-interest-for-18-month-delayed-possession-of-rs-33-lakh-flat/).
- **Why now:** the order landed 27 Aug 2026, inside this sweep's window — and gives Pune, whose registration/RERA slot has sat open across multiple recent loops, its own distinct, dated item rather than a generic city-guide mention.
- **Exclusivity:** LOW-MEDIUM (public regulatory order) — TERREM's synthesis: the number that matters here isn't the ₹33 lakh flat cost, it's the reference date MahaRERA anchored the interest calculation to — the possession date in the *registered* Agreement for Sale, not any later date the builder may have informally cited. That's the checkable fact for any Pune buyer sitting on a delayed handover: your claim runs from what the registered agreement says, not from what you were verbally told.
- **Persona × pain:** P1×DELIVERY (98% of buyers want completion assurance; this is what enforcement of that assurance actually looks like, mechanically)
- **Suggested hook direction:** #2 family (avoidable loss + it was checkable), adapted — "A Pune couple's flat was delayed 18 months past the date in their registered agreement.
