## Signal 1: The government just gave every late builder in India a 4-month excuse — no project-by-project proof required. — REACTIVE — 48h deadline

- **What the data shows:** On 31 Jul 2026, the Union Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) issued an advisory directing all state RERAs to grant a blanket four-month extension to the registration and completion timelines of real-estate projects whose completion date, revised completion date, or extended completion date falls on or after 28 Feb 2026 — invoking Section 6 (force majeure) of the RERA Act on the basis that the West Asia conflict, which the Finance Ministry classified as "war" in April 2026, has disrupted global construction-material supply chains. Critically, the Ministry recommended that state RERAs issue one common order covering all eligible projects, rather than requiring each developer to file and prove an individual force-majeure claim. Developer bodies CREDAI and NAREDCO have welcomed the move. Sources: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/govt-advises-reras-to-extend-real-estate-project-timelines-by-4-months-126073101601_1.html), [Daily Pioneer](https://dailypioneer.com/news/housing-ministry-asks-reras-to-give-4month-extension-to-projects-hit-by-west-asia-conflict), [NewsX](https://www.newsx.com/business/waiting-for-your-dream-home-reras-new-4-month-extension-could-change-your-possession-date-255807/), [NewKerala](https://www.newkerala.com/news/a/govt-advises-state-reras-grant-4-month-extension-real-887.htm), advisory dated 31 Jul 2026.
- **Why now — REACTIVE:** this is a nationwide regulatory policy change to possession deadlines — squarely inside the reactive-lane trigger list ("policy change") — issued three days before this report and still working its way into individual state RERA common orders, meaning the news cycle and the buyer-facing "does my project qualify" question are both live right now, not settled history.
- **Exclusivity:** LOW (public advisory) — TERREM's synthesis: a blanket, no-individual-proof extension is structurally indistinguishable from cover for builders who were already going to miss their date for ordinary reasons. The checkable angle: cross-reference a project's *original* delay history (was it already behind before Feb 2026?) against the new four-month grace window — a project with a clean pre-existing record gets genuine relief; a project with a pattern of prior slippage just got four more months of plausible deniability.
- **Persona × pain:** P1×DELIVERY (98% completion-assurance demand, directly diluted by a blanket excuse), P2×RERA (regulatory-risk widening — force majeure now covers any project system-wide, not case-by-case)

## Signal 5: In Hyderabad, TG-RERA just ruled that giving your land to a builder doesn't make you a homebuyer

Srinivasa Rao, Laxmi Narayana Jannu), reported ~28 Jul 2026, dismissed a landowner's complaint against M/s ISTA Homes Builders & Developers LLP without examining its merits, holding that landowners who enter a Joint Development Agreement (JDA) cannot invoke RERA as "allottees" against the developer. The underlying dispute: Dr BM Naidu and his wife signed a Development Agreement-cum-GPA in Sep 2022 for their 556-sq-yard plot at Guttala Begumpet, Hyderabad, with an agreed project timeline of Oct 2024; they alleged the builder missed that deadline, stopped paying the monthly rental compensation due from Nov 2024, sold two flats before obtaining the Occupancy Certificate, and left common amenities incomplete — all of which TG-RERA left un-adjudicated on the jurisdictional ground alone. Source: [Newsmeter](https://newsmeter.in/hyderabad/can-landowners-drag-builders-to-tg-rera-over-project-delays-telangana-authoritys-ruling-explained-772284), TG-RERA Complaint No. 579 of 2025, reported ~28 Jul 2026.
- **Why now:** a genuinely different violation pattern than any Hyderabad story run in the last three loops (unregistered-project sales, title/GPA resale conflicts) — this is the first ruling this cycle on who RERA protects at all, with direct relevance to Hyderabad's high volume of JDA-based redevelopment given the same builder also sold flats pre-OC, a red flag TG-RERA didn't even have to reach to dismiss the case.
- **Exclusivity:** LOW-MEDIUM (public order) — TERREM's synthesis: most RERA-verification content is written for the buyer of a finished unit; this ruling is the reminder that landowners who fund a project with their land — a common Hyderabad redevelopment structure — have *less* statutory protection than a cash buyer, not more, which is the opposite of what most landowners assume when they sign a JDA.
